**Outlaws and the Deadly Beats** is our game developed for the **GMTK Game Jam 2026**. 

- **Theme:** Countdown 
- **Gênero:** Rhythm
- **Status:** Finished 

## 🎮 About the Game
Have you ever thought of standoffs as rhythmitic? Have you ever wanted to be in one (kinda weird). Well, our game grasps that concept, enemies come from all of the 4 cardinal directions trying to get ya, but since you want do to it stylish, you need to shoot'em right when the timer counts to 0. Enjoy tryng to get the perfect shoot timing.

## ⌨️ How to Play
* **WASD:** Choose the direction and shoot immediately

## 🛠️ Made with
* **Engine:** Unity.
* **Art:** Aseprite.
* **Audio:** Audacity.
